Who We Are

A western liquor house in Edmonton

Sipping Prairies is a western-rooted bottle shop on Fort Road, bringing the easy spirit of the open prairie to Edmonton. From rare whisky to everyday favourites, our shelf is hand-picked and our door is always open with a warm welcome — and our man Mr. Sippy is never far from a good story or a better recommendation.

Mission & Values

and a better bottle.

Our Mission

Sipping Prairies exists to make buying a bottle feel like a visit, not a transaction. We curate a shelf of whisky, wine, and fine spirits worth getting excited about, and pair it with the kind of welcome the prairies are known for.

Whether you’re marking a moment or winding down the evening, we want you to leave with something you’ll love — and a reason to come back.

Core Values

We’re western at heart: warm, honest, and a little playful. We believe in quality over hype, in knowing our shelf and our neighbours by name, and in treating every customer — and every bottle — with care.

We back the people who back our community, and we pour every welcome like we mean it.
